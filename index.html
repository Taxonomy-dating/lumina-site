The problem

Everyone's competing for the same six photos.

The result isn't just shallow matching. It's a professional ecosystem where the same handful of people date the same handful of people, over and over.

01

Signal poverty

A photo says nothing about ambition, intellect or values. And bios are a battlefield — "entrepreneur | traveller | sapiosexual" appears on every third profile in every city.

02

Privacy fear

Half the professionals you'd actually want to meet won't join a dating app, because the colleagues, clients and founders they'd see there are the problem. Not the app.

03

Mismatch fatigue

A PhD researcher and a crypto day-trader match on looks. They meet. In ten minutes they discover they have nothing to say to each other. Repeat, indefinitely.

The engine

Four layers of compatibility.

Deterministic where it counts. Probabilistic only where it can't hurt.

35%

Feature vector

128 dimensions derived from keyword hits across your work and study history. Dense, structural, and honest about what it's measuring.

25%

Bucket affinity

Same bucket scores 1.0, adjacent 0.6, everything else 0.2. The floor is high on purpose — you'd rather see nobody than the wrong crowd.

25%

Quiz compatibility

30 questions across six dimensions, scored with a geometric mean so one mismatched axis drags the whole pair down. Dealbreaker questions cap the score at 0.15.

15%

Preferences

Age range, distance, and the buckets you want to see — applied as hard filters before anything is scored.

The score 
score = 0.35·vector
      + 0.25·bucket
      + 0.25·quiz
      + 0.15·prefs

× authenticity (0.95 – 1.04)
× llmNudge    (0.95 – 1.05)

Both multipliers are boost-only. The engine can promote a good candidate but never bury one — a bad model day can't remove someone you should meet.

OCEAN

Big Five personality, derived from the same 30 answers — so a match is scored on how you actually behave, not just what you want.

DAT

Differential Aptitude Test — divergence, originality and flexibility scored from your open-text answers, keyword-normalised against the corpus.

LIWC

Word-level analysis on your language: how you write is a signal, and the engine reads it for tone, drive and warmth.

LLM

A small local model reads your answers for soft signals. It only ever nudges the rank by 5% — it can never label or filter you.

The privacy contract

The best feature is the one that never renders.

Your job title is the most sensitive thing you own. We read it. We never show it. Not to other users, not to employers, not to anyone.

✕ Access tokens server-side only
  • Read-only by default

    We request the narrowest scopes that exist. Nothing is posted, liked, or messaged on your behalf.

  • Server-side only

    Extraction runs on our servers. Your raw profile data is never sent to a third party — including the AI model, which runs locally on our own hardware.

  • Row-level security

    Every query runs inside a transaction scoped to app.user_id. A pooled connection physically cannot leak one user's context into another's.

  • Consent per platform

    Link or unlink each account individually. Unlink deletes your tokens and your derived snapshot within 24 hours.

  • Erase on request

    Right to erasure under the DPDP Act 2023. One request, everything gone.

  • Signal only, no display

    Your work informs the match. It is never shown. There is no setting, paywall or feature that reveals it — because there is no code path that renders it.
